K Group cuts the prices of some 1,000 everyday brand staples and some 200 popular Pirkka K Group private label products. The price cuts implemented at the beginning of January concern and can be seen in all three K Group grocery store chains. The extent of the price cuts, however, varies depending on the size of the store. &rdquo;Consumer expectations regarding both their own finances and the Finnish economy continue to be muted and the importance of price is still emphasised. K Group has traditionally been strong with campaigns and offers, but now we are making a significant adjustment to our basic price levels. The price cuts concern over 1,200 everyday staples that consumers buy often and that account for a significant portion of sales. Price reductions of branded products are on average 4&ndash;6 percent, but at best, the price cuts can be as much as 15&ndash;20 percent.
