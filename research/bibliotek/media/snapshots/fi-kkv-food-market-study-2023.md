FCCA studies the functioning of the food market - KKV Skip to content Suomi Svenska English Front page Current Issues About FCCA Contacts Search the site Open menu Close menu Front page Current Issues About FCCA Contacts Search the site Suomi Svenska English FCCA studies the functioning of the food market 23.11.2023 News Listen Listen The Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority (FCCA) is currently conducting a study on the structure of food prices and price transmissions, i.e., how changes in, for example, agricultural production costs propagate through the food chain. The structure of prices is examined by means of price margins, which refer to how the consumer price is divided among each stage of production in the food chain. Based on preliminary findings, there are differences between products and product groups, both in terms of price margin changes and price transmissions. The interim report of the study will be published in spring 2024. The prices of food products, like many other products, rose exceptionally fast in 2022. At the same time, it was difficult for producers to shift the increase in input prices to producer prices. The FCCA&#8217;s study examines the functioning of the food market by examining the competitive conditions of the market. The transmission of market signals from one level to another is one of the key features of the functioning of the market. The equilibrium of functioning markets is determined by supply and demand. As circumstances change, the market seeks a new equilibrium through price changes. By finding out how the market will settle in this new equilibrium, it is possible to obtain information on the actual functioning of the market.
