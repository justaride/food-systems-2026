CGR Sweden Services About Dashboard Beta Contact us Building Sweden&#x27;s climate leadership with the circular economy In collaboration with the Research Institutes of Sweden (RISE) and RE:Source Sweden&#x27;s emissions reduction goals are the most ambitious worldwide: net-zero by 2045. But the energy transition is only part of the story. Our Circularity Gap Report Sweden finds that Sweden must—and can—do more to become a true climate champion. The key lies in building its circular economy to shrink its large material footprint. Scroll for the full story or Skip to download the report Swedes consume more than twice as many materials as the global average: 25 tonnes per person, per year While Sweden pursues the vital goal of reducing emissions, another climate threat has gone largely unnoticed: the country&#x27;s high material consumption. We conducted a Circularity Gap Analysis and found that Sweden funnels more than 266 million tonnes of materials into its economy each year . This means that Swedes consume more than twice as many materials as the global average: 25 tonnes per person, per year. 96% of Sweden&#x27;s materials come from virgin sources Only 3.4% of the resources Sweden uses are cycled back into the economy after use. Or in other words: more than 96% of Sweden&#x27;s resources are extracted from virgin sources. In fact, Sweden&#x27;s extraction rates (per capita) are the fourth largest in the world. Such overconsumption exhausts the planet&#x27;s vital ecosystems and threatens our quality of life.
