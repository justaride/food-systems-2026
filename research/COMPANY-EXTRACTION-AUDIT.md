## Workflow recommendation

Treat this as a durable, idempotent workflow:

1. Extract company candidates from DB documents, local text files and PDF catalog.
2. Resolve candidates to Company rows by normalized name, aliases and org number.
3. Promote missing or untracked candidates only after source review.
4. Enrich promoted companies with registry fields, ownership, board, financials and source document links.
5. Re-run this audit and `npx tsx scripts/verify-data-integrity.ts` until gaps are either filled or explicitly waived.

Acceptance criteria for the 231 tracked set:

- Every tracked company has at least one explicit source link in `CompanyDocumentRef`, unless it is registry-only and marked as such in metadata.
- Every tracked company has value-chain stage, ownership type, NACE/category, HQ/country and at least one provenance-bearing detail relation.
- Every high-priority unmatched candidate is either imported, linked to an existing Company row, or waived with reason.
