## How to use this file

This is a claim audit, not a prose draft.

Each claim is split into:

- `Working claim`: the stronger internal formulation
- `Publication-safe language`: the version that can be used in the whitepaper without overclaiming
- `Assessment`: how strong the claim is in the current corpus
- `Supporting evidence`: the main documents that carry the claim
- `Complicating or limiting evidence`: what should hedge or qualify the claim

Assessment levels:

- `strongly-documented`
- `moderately-supported`
- `promising-but-weak`
- `speculative`
