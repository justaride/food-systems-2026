# Project Fit P2 Gap Worklist

Generated: 2026-04-20

- Input pack: `PROJECT-FIT-EVIDENCE-PACK.md`
- Claim matrix: `PROJECT-FIT-CLAIM-MATRIX.md`
- Purpose: fill the current evidence gaps before the next whitepaper synthesis pass

## Why this file exists

The current project-fit pack is already strong on:

- governance and enforcement
- market concentration and access bottlenecks
- operator control of logistics and infrastructure
- preparedness, self-sufficiency, and resilience framing

It is still thinner on four transition themes:

1. side-stream valorization and redistribution
2. nutrient loops and circular input recovery
3. alternative feed and marine / biomass substitution
4. fermentation, mycoprotein, and scale-up barriers

The point of this worklist is not to bulk-load all of P2.

The point is to pull a small, high-yield set of documents that closes those four gaps without overloading the corpus with weak landing pages, repeated bankruptcy news, or generic advocacy material.

## Working decision

The strongest fillers are not evenly distributed across P2.

- `04_Food_Waste_And_Circularity` is the best source for side-streams, redistribution, and some nutrient-loop material.
- `07_Academic_Research_And_Theses` is the best source for mechanism evidence and method discipline.
- `05_Foodtech_Alt_Protein_And_Innovation` is useful mainly for regulatory context, ecosystem mapping, incumbent feed actors, and a controlled set of scale-failure cases.

## What not to bulk-load

Do not treat these as first-wave evidence:

- browser-printed company homepages such as `PROMYC`, `Foodsharing Copenhagen`, `PeelPioneers`, and `NPHarvest`
- multiple news rewrites covering the same bankruptcy event
- advocacy-facing PDFs from `Framtiden i våre hender` when a policy, audit, or thesis source exists for the same claim

Those documents can still be useful later for examples, public framing, or case color. They just should not set the argument.

## Operational implication

For the next database or synthesis step, the right move is:

1. reuse already-linked thesis / duplicate material first
2. promote the missing structural P2 documents second
3. add only a narrow band of company and failure cases third

That keeps the corpus evidence-led instead of news-led.
