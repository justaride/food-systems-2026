# Assessing Amino Acid Solubility of Black Soldier Fly Larvae Meal in Atlantic Salmon
**Journal:** Frontiers in Physiology, 2022
**PMCID:** PMC9727232

### Abstract
This study evaluated the amino acid solubility of Black Soldier Fly (BSF) larvae meal and experimental diets for Atlantic salmon.

**Key Results:**
* The in vivo true protein digestibility of BSF larvae meal was exceptionally high at 99%.
* Strong correlation between in vitro and in vivo results when using species-specific enzymes.
* BSF larvae meal is a highly digestible and promising alternative protein source.
