# Alternative Protein Sources in Aquafeed: Current Scenario and Future Perspectives
**Journal:** Veterinary and Animal Science, 2024
**PMID:** 39280774

### Abstract
Fish meal represents the main protein source for most commercially farmed aquatic species. This review examines the use of fish meal and alternative protein sources on the health, welfare, and growth performance.

**Key Findings:**
* Plant-derived sources (10% to 80%) and animal-derived sources (2% to 100%) were tested.
* Replacements generally generated positive effects on health, welfare, and growth.
* Future outlook: Blends of different protein sources.
