# Meld. St. 27 (2019–2020) – Chapter 2: Daglegvare og konkurranse

**Source:** [Regjeringen.no](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/meld.-st.-27-20192020/id2714670?ch=2)

## Summary
This section analyzes the competition dynamics in the Norwegian grocery market.

### Key Points:
* **Market Structure:** The market is highly concentrated, dominated by three major groups (NorgesGruppen, Coop, and Reitangruppen).
* **Competition Factors:** Price competition is intense, especially in the discount segment, but high store density and limited access to attractive locations act as barriers to entry for new players.
* **Buyer Power:** The large grocery chains have significant power over suppliers, which can lead to lower prices for consumers but may also suppress innovation or exclude smaller suppliers.
* **Vertical Integration:** Chains are increasingly integrated, owning their own wholesalers and private labels (EMV), which strengthens their market position.
* **Government Actions:** The government proposes a permanent focus on the grocery sector within the Norwegian Competition Authority (Konkurransetilsynet) and plans to map profitability and corporate structures to ensure fair competition.
