# Meld. St. 25 (2024–2025) – Chapter 10: Et matsystem for framtiden

**Source:** [Regjeringen.no](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/meld.-st.-25-20242025/id3095592?ch=10)

## Summary
This chapter discusses the future of the Norwegian food system from a climate perspective.

### Key Points:
* **System Overview:** The food system encompasses primary production, processing, distribution, marketing, sales, and consumption. It is responsible for 21–37% of global greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Climate Goals:** Norway aims to be a low-emission society by 2050. While some emissions from food production are expected to remain, the government seeks to reduce them across the entire value chain while ensuring food security and self-sufficiency.
* **Holistic Policy:** The policy integrates human, animal, and environmental health (the "One Health" approach).
* **Strategies:** The government has launched a strategy to increase self-sufficiency of agricultural products to 50% (on an energy basis). It also uses the "Avoid, Shift, Improve" (UFF) framework to create a more sustainable system.
* **Specific Sectors:**
    * **Agriculture:** Focuses on the 2019 climate agreement with agricultural organizations, soil health, and reducing emissions at the farm level (e.g., through biogas and new technology).
    * **Fisheries and Aquaculture:** Aims to reduce the climate footprint of seafood, address emissions from feed, and transition the fishing fleet to green energy.
    * **Food Waste:** Norway is committed to halving food waste by 2030. A new "Food Waste Law" (matsvinnlov) is proposed for 2025.
