# Prop. 4 L (2025–2026) – Chapter 2: Bakgrunnen for lovforslaget

**Source:** [Regjeringen.no](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/prop.-4-l-20252026/id3124887?ch=2)

## Summary
This document proposes changes to the enforcement of the Act on Good Trading Practices.

### Key Points:
* **Current Status:** The Act on Good Trading Practices entered into force on January 1, 2021.
* **Challenges with the Grocery Authority:** Since its inception, the dedicated Grocery Authority (Dagligvaretilsynet) has faced several issues:
    * It is a small, vulnerable organization with high turnover.
    * It has struggled to recruit relevant legal expertise in its Porsgrunn location.
    * It has received fewer formal complaints than expected, leading to limited enforcement and development of legal standards.
* **Proposed Change:** The government proposes to abolish the independent Grocery Authority and move the responsibility for enforcing the act to the Norwegian Competition Authority (Konkurransetilsynet).
* **International Context:** The document notes how similar regulations are enforced in other countries, such as the UK (GCA), Sweden (Konkurrensverket), and Denmark (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen).
* **Goal:** The move is intended to create a more robust and efficient enforcement environment by leveraging the existing expertise and resources of the Competition Authority.
