# Prop. 33 L (2019–2020) – Chapter 2: Bakgrunnen for lovforslaget

**Source:** [Regjeringen.no](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/prop.-33-l-20192020/id2681097?ch=2)

## Summary
This chapter provides the background for the "Act on Good Trading Practices in the Grocery Supply Chain".

### Key Points:
* **Historical Context:** Since the 1980s, the industry has shifted from small, independent retailers to large, integrated chains.
* **Need for Regulation:** Investigations (e.g., NOU 2011: 4 and NOU 2013: 6) revealed dissatisfaction with negotiation practices, unclear contracts, and unilateral changes to agreements.
* **Core Principles:** The proposed law is based on honesty, predictability, and loyalty. It aims to facilitate efficient negotiations and contracts without dictating commercial outcomes.
* **Consumer Interest:** By making negotiations more efficient, the government expects the resulting gains to be passed on to consumers through competition.
* **Enforcement:** The proposal includes the establishment of a dedicated Grocery Authority (Dagligvaretilsynet) to enforce the act.
