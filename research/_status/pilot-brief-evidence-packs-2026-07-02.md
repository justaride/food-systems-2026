## Claim boundaries

- The Finnish §4a brief frames the 30 percent rule as a dominance-assessment shortcut, not an automatic infringement.
- The logistics brief uses Menon/Kvale as a cautionary source because the report does not recommend functional/accounting separation on its reviewed evidence.
- The zoning brief treats municipal HHI as a screening proxy, not a proof of unlawful exclusion.
- The ASKO/Vestby brief supports scenario modeling only; it does not claim a proven single point of failure.
- The procurement brief treats Copenhagen as a mechanism benchmark with kitchen-capability and waste-reduction components, not as proof that procurement clauses alone drive transition.
