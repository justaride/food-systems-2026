# R13-WASTE-008 source-shortlist control — 2026-07-02

## Scope

Controlled `research/external/r13/R13-WASTE-008-prevention-baseline.md` for prevention-oriented food-waste interventions, baseline requirements and measurement caveats.

## Checks

- Checked NORSUS/Matvett OR.27.25 `Faktaark om matsvinn i Norge 2024` for 2024 baseline, kg/inhabitant reductions and sector coverage.
- Checked Matvett/NORSUS servering signal for the 30.6% gram-per-guest reduction boundary and the weaker kg/inhabitant framing.
- Checked Prop. 130 L and Mattilsynet hearing material for the Matsvinnloven timing: adopted 2025-06-20, but effect measurement is not available and regulations still define practical entry into force.
- Checked JRC133003 as a method-guard source: intervention evidence is heterogeneous and isolated effect evaluation is often missing.
- Checked Nord 2024:034 as Nordic comparison/method context, not as a harmonized intervention-effect table.

## Result

`R13-WASTE-008` is moved from `source-shortlist-ready` to `source-shortlist-controlled`.

Controlled anchors:

- NORSUS/Matvett reports 407 100 tonnes of mapped Norwegian food waste in 2024 and 73.4 kg per inhabitant.
- The 2015-2024 Norwegian reduction is an aggregate sector/program signal, not an isolated single-intervention effect.
- Dagligvare and servering have stronger baseline/method series than household- or intervention-specific prevention claims.

No claim-lock was created. Isolated effect per prevention measure remains a B/C cell unless a future PCQ establishes baseline, method, scope and attribution.
