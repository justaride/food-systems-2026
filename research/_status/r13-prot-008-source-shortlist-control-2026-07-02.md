# R13-PROT-008 source-shortlist control — 2026-07-02

## Scope

Controlled `research/external/r13/R13-PROT-008-bonner-erter-akerbonne.md` for Norwegian cultivation of beans, peas and faba beans: area, aggregate deliveries, food/feed boundary, processing chain and open gaps.

## Checks

- Checked NIBIO/Lundby source signal for 2024 area: about 48 000 daa faba beans and 38 000 daa peas, with about 10 000 daa as the 2013 reference point.
- Checked Landbruksdirektoratet Rapport 3-16/2026 full PDF for food-industry use, barrier logic, possible subsidy design and source caveats.
- Checked Felleskjøpet `sluttrapport 2024/25`, sourced to Landbruksdirektoratet, for aggregate `Erter og bønner` delivery volume: 24 342 tonnes in 2024/25.
- Checked Felleskjøpet kornguide 2025/26 for area subsidy and delivery-agreement price signals.
- Checked SSB metadata for tables 07479, 10508, 11506 and 07495. Prior reference to 07495 was stale/wrong: it is a school-points table, not agriculture.
- Checked Nofima and NMBU/Forskning sources as process and agronomy context only, not realized-volume proof.

## Result

`R13-PROT-008` is moved from `source-shortlist-ready` to `source-shortlist-controlled`.

Controlled anchors:

- 2024 area: about 48 000 daa faba beans and 38 000 daa peas, from NIBIO/Lundby.
- 2024/25 aggregate delivery: 24 342 tonnes `Erter og bønner`, from Felleskjøpet/Landbruksdirektoratet.
- Food-use boundary: Landbruksdirektoratet 2026 estimates up to 1 000 tonnes annual human-consumption use of peas and faba beans combined, but this is an indicative industry-dialogue estimate.
- Policy/pricing boundary: 2025/26 kornguide gives 505 kr/daa area subsidy and delivery-agreement increments, but those are not production claims.

No claim-lock was created. Separate crop volumes, food/feed split, food-quality share and national processing volume remain C-cells unless Landbruksdirektoratet/Felleskjøpet/kornmottak or another primary source provides a clean split.
