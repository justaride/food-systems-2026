# CAR-008 Regenerative, Local and Small-Scale Practice Actor Memo

Status: first-pass data collection
Date: 2026-06-24
Scope: market gardens, CSA/andelslandbruk, REKO, seed networks, forest garden/agroforestry context and small-scale local value-chain actors.

## What Was Collected

This batch enriched existing R12/CAR practice candidates and added a small set of actor-specific CSA/seed/network rows: REKO Norge, Markedshager Norge, Smaskala Gront Norge, Okoguiden andelslandbruk candidates, KVANN, KVANN skoghage kartlegging, Solhatt, Root2Fork, Norsk genressurssenter, Vestern Andelslandbruk and Anda Andelsgard.

## Strongest Findings

KVANN, Solhatt and REKO Norge have the strongest identity-source rows, but they are still not verified production-impact rows. The practice-category universe remains actor-gated because directories and maps are not audited active-producer registers.

## Key Gaps

- Current season evidence for CSA/farm rows.
- Actor-owned locators for individual market gardens.
- Open membership lists for Smaskala Gront Norge and KVANN.
- Separation between network, producer, advisory and research rows.

## Import Recommendation

No CAR-008 rows should be imported as full verified producer rows from this pass alone. Use the batch for candidate enrichment, source-shortlist and context.

## Ikke-Si Rules Added By This Batch

- Do not treat directory counts as verified active farm totals.
- Do not make networks into producers.
- Do not infer regenerative practice from general local-food or organic-source language.
