# CAR-011 Failure, Dormant, Bankruptcy and Survival Actor Memo

Status: first-pass failure/survival data collection
Date: 2026-06-24
Scope: dormant, restructured, acquired, bankrupt and survival cases relevant to circular food, alternative protein, side-stream, CEA and food-system networks.

## What Was Collected

Rows include Norwegian Gruten, Gront fra Nord, Ecoprot legacy, Pronofa survival/update and EAT Foundation wind-down, plus Nordic/global context cases ENORM BioFactory, Mycorena, Infarm, Ljusgarda, DUG Foodtech, Hooked Foods and Stockeld Dreamery.

## Strongest Findings

Gruten has actor-primary wind-down evidence but unresolved legal entity matching. Gront fra Nord is a reconstruction case, not a bankruptcy case. Mycorena preserves both bankruptcy filing and acquisition context. EAT is an announced wind-down while Brreg still shows active/not bankrupt.

## Key Gaps

- Official legal/court registry proof for some Nordic failure rows.
- Clean Ecoprot legal identity.
- Whether Infarm global status should be split by legal entity/country.
- Secondary-only Swedish evidence-pack cases need official register follow-up.

## Import Recommendation

Use this batch primarily as context/failure learning rows. Pronofa remains a verified-ready active row but must not inherit current insect-production claims from Ecoprot. Do not call reconstruction, wind-down or acquisition the same thing as bankruptcy.

## Ikke-Si Rules Added By This Batch

- Do not treat bankruptcy as proof that a technology cannot work.
- Do not erase successor/acquisition status after bankruptcy.
- Do not call EAT or Gront fra Nord bankrupt based on wind-down/reconstruction evidence.
- Do not import founder/person data from secondary failure coverage.
