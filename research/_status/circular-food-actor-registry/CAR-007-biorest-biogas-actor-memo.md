## What Was Collected

This batch split combined seed rows into legal/facility rows where sources allowed it: Lindum AS, Greve Biogass AS, Den Magiske Fabrikken AS, Cambi ASA, Romerike biogassanlegg, ROAF, IVAR IKS, Ecopro AS, Mjosanlegget AS, BIR Bioressurs AS, Attvin Naering AS, Bokashi Norge AS, VermiNord AS and AquaPro AS.

## Strongest Findings

Lindum, Greve Biogass, Den Magiske Fabrikken, IVAR, Ecopro, Mjosanlegget and BIR Bioressurs have the strongest source-backed mechanism rows. Cambi is a strong support/enabling actor but should not be treated as a processor. ROAF and Attvin are collection/route candidates, not verified plant operators. AquaPro remains parked.
