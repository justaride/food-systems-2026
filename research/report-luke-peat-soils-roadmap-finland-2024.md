## Relevans (Relevance)
P1-kilde for Finland. Dokumenterer hvorfor torvjord er den avgjorende finske matsystemutfordringen og gir konkrete reduksjonspotensialer.
## Hovedfunn (Key Findings)
- Peat soils account for over half of Finland agricultural greenhouse gas emissions.
- Organic agricultural soils produce 50-60 prosent of agricultural sector greenhouse gas emissions while covering about 10 prosent of the total agricultural area.
- Luke published a 2024 roadmap for climate-smart use of peatlands with two pathways to 2050.
- The environmental pathway could reduce peat-soil emissions by more than 28 prosent, equivalent to 2.2 MtCO2e; the production-oriented pathway could reduce them by nearly 17 prosent, or 1.3 MtCO2e.
## Anbefalinger (Recommendations)
- Use peat soils as the central Finnish agriculture-climate indicator in Nordic comparisons.
- Track rewetting, wetland creation, controlled drainage, perennial crops and permanent grassland/fallow as mitigation measures.
- Separate peat-soil emissions from narrower agriculture-sector totals when comparing Finland with other countries.
