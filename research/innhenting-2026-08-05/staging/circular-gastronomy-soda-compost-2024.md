# Soda from compost waste? Why not! (kitchen scraps to soft drinks)
Circular Gastronomy Challenge case; winner announced 2025-01-27 (listed year 2024). Actors: Törst (founded 2020) + eSmiley + Gram Group.

Figures (actor-claims):
- Prototype: 20-litre barrel made in ~3 hours.
- Operational scale ~2,000 litres per batch, yielding ~6,000 bottles of "Kompostläsk".
- Gram Group runs 3 hotels and 6 restaurants on Bjäre peninsula, SW Sweden; freezes food waste until target volume reached.
No measured food-waste volumes, conversion ratios, or environmental metrics.
