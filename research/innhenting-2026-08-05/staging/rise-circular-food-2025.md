## Project: "Circular food in practice: Collaboration for sustainable public meals" (RISE, 2025)
- Explores how public meals can become more sustainable through circular methods and raw
  materials that also work in times of crisis.
- Lead: RISE, with the Board of Agricultural Education – Västra Götaland Region and the
  University of Borås; in collaboration with the School Meals Academy, the Climate 2030
  initiative, Switch Food Hub Sweden, and the Food Accelerator.
- Context figure (aktoropplysning): the Swedish public meal system serves "3 million meals a
  day" to pupils, hospital patients, and the elderly.
- Goals: regional network + catalyst for pilots/tools/recommendations that strengthen local
  food supply, reduce environmental/climate impact of public-sector food, and develop a roadmap
  for circular food systems in Västra Götaland. No budget/quantitative targets found.
