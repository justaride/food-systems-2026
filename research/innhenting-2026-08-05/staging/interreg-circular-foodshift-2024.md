# Circular FoodShift
Interreg Baltic Sea Region 2021-2027. Timeframe Aug 2023 – Jul 2025. Budget EUR 0.50 million total (EUR 0.40 million ERDF).

Lead: Association of Municipalities of Tartu County (EE). Partners: Sustainable Gastro (LT), Latvian Rural Advisory and Training Centre (LV), Green Liberty (LV), Seinäjoki University of Applied Sciences (FI).
Outputs (project-reported):
- >40 procurement specialists/agencies/producers/municipal reps engaged.
- "Making the School Community Food-Wise": 5 schools in Tartu County, +5 more from autumn 2025.
- 43 chefs/catering professionals trained in Latvia.
- 40 educators (EE/LV/LT/FI) via webinars; 36 organisations benefited.
No tonnage/percentage waste-reduction figures reported.
