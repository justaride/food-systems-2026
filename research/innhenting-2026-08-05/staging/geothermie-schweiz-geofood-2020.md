# Geothermal energy for circular food production (GEOFOOD)
Geothermie-Schweiz, published 2020-09-28. GEOFOOD project across Iceland, Slovenia, Netherlands. Institutions: Wageningen UR (Bleiswijk, NL), Samraekt (Reykjavik), Agricultural University of Iceland, Landsvirkjun.

Figures (measured/reported statistics):
- Europe produces 1.5 GWe electricity from geothermal; estimated capacity 80-100 GWe.
- Iceland: 663 MWe from high-temperature fields (~1/3 of national electricity).
- Direct use for food production and industries ~9% of current geothermal utilisation in Iceland.
- Netherlands 2019: 21 geothermal doublets operational, supplying 5,600 TJ heat (mostly commercial greenhouses).
Project builds decoupled aquaponics (RAS + horticulture) demo units; no measured yields/species outputs given.
