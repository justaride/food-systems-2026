# Amcor backs Danish circular food packaging recycling project (CRISP)
New Food Magazine, published 2025-12-23.

CRISP (Circular Recycling Innovation for Sustainable Packaging): 2025-2028, lead Danish Technological Institute; partners incl. Amcor (facility in Randers, DK), food manufacturers, waste specialists. Goal: recycle rigid HDPE/PP household food packaging into new food-grade applications in Denmark. Funding amount not disclosed.
Figures:
- Amcor CleanStream (Leamington Spa, UK) "can recycle nearly 40 percent of all domestically collected PP waste" — actor-claim.
- EU target: 55% plastic recycling rate by 2030 — regulatory target.
No project-specific tonnage/targets.
