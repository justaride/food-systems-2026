## Extracted argument (Prof. Leif Hem, NHH)

- Core: food producers should bypass grocery chains by selling directly to consumers online. "Sell the brands directly! If they do not pass this test, then perhaps their products are not up to scratch."
- Market concentration: "Norway only has three large supermarket chains, which is a cause for concern for suppliers."
- Power asymmetry: "Primary producers have to both collaborate with the chains as partners and compete with them as rivals."
- Private labels: comprise "at least 13–14% of the total turnover," rising to ~30% in categories like fresh meat and produce. Grocery sales ~160 billion NOK annually.
- Chain tactics: cross-subsidization; chains may sell own-brands "at such a low price that they actually lose money on the sale" while using supplier profits to offset losses.
- Solution: direct-to-consumer online platforms (marked.no, Kolonial.no) could undercut chains — "they will be able to offer their products at a lower price than the chains."
