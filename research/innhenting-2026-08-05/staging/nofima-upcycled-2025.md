**Title:** A Perspective of Upcycled Food by Norwegian Consumers: The Meaning Without the Word
**Authors:** Miriam Garcia Valencia, Kristine Myhrer Svartebekk, Themistoklis Altintzoglou, Mari Øvrum Gaarder
**Year:** 2025
**Journal:** Journal of International Food & Agribusiness Marketing, pp. 1–25

## Abstract (extracted)
The study examines how Norwegian consumers conceptualize upcycled food, which lacks a direct
translation in Norwegian. Through focus group discussions, researchers identified 76 words and
concepts associated with upcycled food, with "sustainability" being the predominant connection.

## Key findings
- Sample: 76 words and concepts were linked to upcycled food (qualitative focus-group study).
- Four primary themes emerged: Culture, Resource Utilization, Innovation, and Ultra-processed —
  interconnected through five codes: "Already done," "Food waste," "Acceptance/change of times,"
  "Lack of knowledge," and "Honesty from food producers."
- First investigation into consumer understanding of upcycled food based purely on its definition;
  insights for producers to use the concept as a marketing approach for circular food systems.
