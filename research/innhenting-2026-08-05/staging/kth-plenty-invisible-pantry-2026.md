# Making Use of the Invisible Pantry
KTH PLENTY profile of Louise Bartek (postdoc, KTH PLENTY), published 2026-03-31.

Profile/interview. "Invisible pantry" = surplus food and by-products that remain underused within food systems. Method: life-cycle assessment (LCA). Qualitative statement: "We produce enormous amounts of food, yet a large share is lost before it even reaches the plate." No quantitative figures in the article.
