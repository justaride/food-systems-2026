# When citrus peels work just as well (citrus upcycling)
Circular Gastronomy Challenge case; winner announced 2025-01-27 (listed year 2024). Actor: Simply No Waste, Skåne/Malmö.

Figures (actor-claims):
- 5 tons of avocado pits saved from restaurants/cafés in Skåne since 2022.
- Planned capacity: 20 tons of citrus shells per month at Malmö facility.
Process: electrical-pulse tech breaks down citrus molecules into flavour enhancers, colorants, texture improvers (plant-based). Inputs: citrus peels from cafés, restaurants, juice industry. No LCA/substitution data.
