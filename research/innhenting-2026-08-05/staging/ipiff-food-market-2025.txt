   IX. Most promising market segments

       • When asked to identify the most promising market segments for 2025, respondents indicated that
       food products such as protein bars and biscuits are considered the most promising. Additionally,
       segments like bread and similar baked goods, functional foods, and meat-like products are viewed as
       quite promising.
       • In 2023, insect food producers tended to focus on product specialization rather than selling whole
       insects, despite the latter maintaining a strong position as a market segment in the near future.
       • By 2030, emerging market segments such as specialty food ingredients (e.g., sports nutrition and
       food supplements) and meat-like food products are expected to gain increasing importance.
