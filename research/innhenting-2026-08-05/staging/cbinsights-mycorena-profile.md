About Mycorena
Mycorena focuses on producing fungi-based alternative proteins for the food industry. It offers a meat-like texture and neutral taste, designed to serve as a sustainable alternative to traditional animal and plant-based proteins. The company's innovative solutions cater to the growing demand for environmentally friendly and sustainable food products. It was founded in 2017 and is based in Gothenburg, Sweden. In July 2024 Mycorena filed for bankruptcy.

Nature's Fynd
Nature's Fynd is a biotechnology company that develops and manufactures solutions using extremophiles, a type of fungi, across various sectors. The company provides products including specialty ingredients for the food and beverage industry, oral biologic drugs, and bio-based performance materials that mimic leather and composites. Nature's Fynd serves the food and beverage supply chain, biopharmaceutical industry, and material science sectors. It was founded in 2012 and is based in Chicago, Illinois.
Quorn Foods
Quorn Foods specializes in the production of vegetarian and vegan food products within the food industry. The company offers a range of meat-free products such as sausages, nuggets, mince, fillets, and burgers, designed to provide meal options without the use of animal products. Quorn Foods caters to consumers seeking sustainable and meat-free dietary choices. It was founded in 1985 and is based in Stokesley, England. Quorn Foods operates as a subsidiary of Monde Nissin.
MycoTechnology
MycoTechnology specializes in food ingredients using fungal fermentation and operates within the food technology and culinary science sectors. The company offers natural flavor enhancers, plant-based proteins, and sweeteners developed through mushroom mycelia fermentation for the taste, texture, and nutritional value of foods. MycoTechnology primarily serves the food and beverage industry, providing solutions for flavor modulation, sugar reduction, and the creation of plant-based alternatives. It was founded in 2013 and is based in Aurora, Colorado.
