# World's first circular fish farm incoming — on land (Onnest, Hirtshals)
FoodNavigator, published 2025-11-10. Location: Hirtshals, Denmark. Founders Samuel Muren & Håkon Volden; architecture by Lendager (CEO Nicholas Duxbury Ransome).

Figures (company/project claims, unverified):
- Fish survival target "over 95%" vs "up to 50%" mortality in typical land-based systems.
- "zero waste"/"fully circular operations"; "zero mortality" ambition; no medication/vaccines; minimal resource use.
- Local algae-based feed replaces imported soy; excess industrial heat (from nearby hydrogen production) powers algae.
Timeline: construction late 2026/early 2027, production launch 2028. Investment and capacity not disclosed.
