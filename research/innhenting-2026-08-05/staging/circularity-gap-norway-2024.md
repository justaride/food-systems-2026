# Circularity Gap Report — Norway

Retrieved: 2026-08-05 via WebFetch (verbatim figure extraction)

## Key figures (verbatim from page)
- Circularity Metric: **2% circular in 2022**, with "0% change since 2018".
  (NB: manifest row titled this "2.4 percent circular" — the live page states 2%.)
- Domestic extraction: **61 tonnes per capita in 2022** (5.1X continental avg.)
- Material footprint: **39 tonnes per capita in 2022**, "5.4% change since 2018" (2.6X continental avg.)
- Total material footprint grew from **197 million tonnes to 208 million tonnes — a 6% increase** (2018–2022)
- Carbon footprint: 16 tonnes per capita in 2021 ("0% change since 2018"); total 87 million tonnes CO2e
- Recycling rate: 38% in 2021 ("2% change since 2018"); 10% lower than continental avg.
