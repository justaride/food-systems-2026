# Pantries below the surface (marine allotments)
Circular Gastronomy Challenge case; winner announced 2024-01-27. Winner: Maria Bodin, University of Gothenburg (Center for Ocean and Society, Dept of Marine Sciences).

Figures (all actor-claims):
- Only 25% of seafood Swedes eat comes from Swedish waters; 75% imported.
- ~30 marine allotments exist in Denmark today.
- ~13 years since Danish authorities adapted regulations for marine allotments.
- Mussel cultivation to consumption size ~18 months.
- Seaweed most protein-rich when harvested March-April.
Concept: small-scale shared marine farms for oysters, mussels, seaweed with community/educational components.
