## What the Order does
Remedy from the UK Competition Commission's groceries market investigation (final report 30 Apr 2008): restricts large grocery retailers' ability to use restrictive covenants and exclusivity arrangements on land to block competitors from opening rival grocery stores (barrier-to-entry / land-banking remedy).

## Key provisions (from WebFetch of the Order)
- Designated Large Grocery Retailers (Schedule 1): Asda, Co-operative Group, Marks & Spencer, Morrisons, Sainsbury's, Tesco, Waitrose (plus agents/subsidiaries with >25% common ownership).
- Restrictive covenants: a large retailer must "use its best endeavours to release" covenants restricting grocery retail (Art. 4(1)); may not impose new covenants that "may restrict grocery retailing or have equivalent effect" (Art. 5(1)).
- Exclusivity arrangements: stop enforcing existing exclusivity agreements within five years (Art. 7(1)); new arrangements capped at five years from store opening (Art. 8).
- Geographic competition test (Schedule 4): a "10-minute drive-time" isochrone; where a retailer controls multiple stores with ≥60% market share in a concentrated local market (≤3 competing fascias), covenants/exclusivity fail the test and must be released.
