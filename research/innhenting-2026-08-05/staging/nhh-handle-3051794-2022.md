## Abstract (verbatim excerpt)
"The prevalent view of excessive profits in Norwegian grocery chains ... This thesis aims to convert perception to the corresponding financial figures, comparing them with Norway's neighboring Scandinavian countries. ... analyzing five to ten years' worth of financial data from the three largest actors in each country. ... Analyzing the price development of food and non-alcoholic beverages in Scandinavia, we found that Norway had the steepest decline in prices. From August 2021 to August 2022, we found that the price growth in Denmark and Sweden were significantly higher than in Norway. Gross margins vary considerably between different grocery chains ... more correlated with the negotiating power with food suppliers ... We found that the largest actors have the highest gross margins. ..."
