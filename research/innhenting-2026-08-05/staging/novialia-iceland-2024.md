# Local food and fibres in Iceland: more than smelly shark and sweaters
Novia University blog, author Ulrika Dahlberg, 2024-10-14.

Figures (actor-claims/observed):
- Istex processes ~99% of all wool in Iceland; Icelandic farmers own >80% of Istex.
- Hand-knitted sweaters priced ~250-450 EUR (observed).
- Iceland "not even close to self-sufficient" in vegetables (Amber Monroe).
No quantitative data on seaweed/microgreens/aquaponic output or import percentages. Author notes limited time for data collection.
