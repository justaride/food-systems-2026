# Stockmann to sell its Delicatessen (Herkku) business in Finland to S Group
Source URL: https://news.cision.com/lindex-group-oyj/r/stockmann-to-sell-its-delicatessen-business-in-finland-to-s-group---stockmann-delicatessen-stores-wi,c2299831
Retrieved: 2026-08-05 via curl (browser UA; WebFetch got 403). Stock Exchange Release, Stockmann plc, 30 June 2017 08:30 EET.

## Facts (verbatim)
- Stockmann plc and S Group signed an agreement to sell Stockmann Delicatessen business operations in Finland to S Group's regional cooperatives. Baltic Delicatessen operations remain with Stockmann.
- Transaction includes all Stockmann Delicatessen business operations in Finland. **Six Delicatessen stores** transferred to HOK-Elanto, Turku and Tampere Regional Cooperatives. Chain operations → SOK; Delicatessen kitchen (Stockmann Meals) → Meira Nova (SOK subsidiary).
- Stockmann Delicatessen has **around 800 employees** in Finland; they transfer to S Group as existing employees.
- Debt-free transaction price: **EUR 27 million**, paid in cash at closing; positive cash-flow effect ~EUR 20 million; one-time capital gain ~EUR 3 million. Transaction improves Stockmann Group profitability from 2018.
- Subject to Finnish Competition and Consumer Authority approval; if granted in 2017, completion 31 December 2017.
- Cited driver: "Changes particularly in the food procurement and logistics market in Finland" (an incumbent grocery-format exit / failed-entrant signal).
