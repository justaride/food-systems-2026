# Circularity Gap Report — Sweden
Source URL: https://www.circularity-gap.world/sweden
Retrieved: 2026-08-05 via WebFetch (verbatim figure extraction)
Institution: Circle Economy / Circularity Gap Reporting Initiative

## Key figures (verbatim from page)
- Circularity Metric: **3.4% (2022)**; "Circularity Gap of more than 96%". 6.4% lower than continental avg.
- Resources into the economy: "More than **266 million tonnes** of resources are funnelled into the economy each year", "nearly **25 tonnes per person**".
- Material footprint: **25 tonnes per capita** (12 tonnes higher than global average).
- Domestic extraction: **26 tonnes per capita** (13 tonnes higher than global average); "tops 265 million tonnes annually — the fourth largest worldwide, per capita".
- Carbon footprint: 7 tonnes per capita (1 tonne higher than global average).
- Value gap: 19% (2025).
