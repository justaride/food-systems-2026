## Facts (verbatim / tight paraphrase)
- Funding: **€26 million** (≈ US$29.7M). Investors: Maki.vc, Firstminute Capital, Springvest, The Finnish Climate Fund, Finnvera, Norion Bank, South Ostrobothnia ELY Centre, Business Finland.
- New plant "Volare 01" in **Pori, Finland** (brownfield site); planned / under construction, not yet operational.
- Planned output: "protein equivalent to **200 million Baltic herrings annually**, roughly **18% of Finland's total commercial fish catch** in protein terms" — PLANNED capacity, not current.
- Existing Hyvinkää facility (opened 2024): "capable of processing **5,000 tonnes of food industry side streams annually**".
- Feedstock: food-industry byproducts / side streams, converted via black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens), closed-loop → protein, oil, fertiliser.
- Efficiency claims: "emissions up to 4–8x lower than soy"; "30% less processing energy"; aims to cut hygienization energy 50%.
- Company plan: 10 production plants by 2030; targeting 3 million tonnes annual CO2 mitigation by 2035.

## Boundary note
- 5,000 t/yr (Hyvinkää) = actual processing CAPACITY of existing plant (aktøropplysning). "200M herring equivalent" = planned/target output of Volare 01 (not yet built). These are permission/capacity/target figures, NOT measured throughput.
