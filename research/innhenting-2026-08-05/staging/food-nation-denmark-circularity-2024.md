# The Danish Agriculture and Food Sector Steps Up Regarding Circularity
Food Nation Denmark. (Article references a May 2022 event; listed year 2024. Date ambiguous.)

Company claims:
- Jalm&B: bread from surplus IPA-beer products and excess "fermentate" water (no quantities).
- Circular Food Technology: upcycles brewery spent grain into protein/fibre-rich ingredients (no quantities).
- Peter Larsen Coffee: currently "less than 1% of the many nutrients of the coffee plant are utilised" (actor-claim).
- Reduced: broth from surplus goods (no quantities).
- PhosphorCare: recycles phosphorus from wastewater into fertiliser (no quantities).
- Carlsberg: 100% recycled plastic on can wraps; recycled plastic "reduces the CO2 footprint by 60%" vs traditional (actor-claim).
