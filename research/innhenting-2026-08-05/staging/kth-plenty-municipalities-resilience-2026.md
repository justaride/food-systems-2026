# A framework to analyze municipalities' capacities in planning for food systems resilience
KTH PLENTY news, published 2026-04-01. Research by Malin Andersson (PhD student, Uppsala University).

Proposes analysing municipal food-system resilience through three resilience capacities: absorptive (coping with shocks), adaptive (adjusting to change), transformative (fundamentally reshaping). Evaluated across seven factors incl. collaboration, learning, supportive structures, power dynamics. Framework tested with data from a Swedish municipality. Municipalities "often lack the tools and capacity" for food-system planning. Qualitative framework; no quantitative outcome statistics on landing page.
