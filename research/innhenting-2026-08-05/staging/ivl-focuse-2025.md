# FOCUSE — Food production and provisioning through Circular Urban Systems in European Cities
IVL research-project page. Funding: Horizon Europe via Driving Urban Transition + Formas. Timeframe 2024-2026. Budget EUR 1,096,456.

Project lead: IVL Swedish Environmental Research Institute. Partners: University of Bologna, Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, KTH, Stockholm Business Region, Nära Sverige, University of Wroclaw, Wroclaw Miasto municipality, Municipality of Skellefteå. Cities: Stockholm, Skellefteå (SE), Barcelona (ES), Bologna (IT), Wroclaw (PL). Swedish partners focus on integrated/indoor agriculture (vertical farms, mushroom farming, rooftop farms). No quantitative circularity targets/volumes specified.
