Title: Local food entrepreneurs in Norway: case studies on successful practices for network learning and market development
Author: Stine Therese Alm Hersleth (supervisors/co-authors Rukke, Kubberød, Gonera). Year: 2023.

## Extracted from abstract
- Local food/drink market sales in Norway: 11.5 billion NOK in 2022 (source cited: Norwegian Food Foundation / Matmerk, 2022).
- "the food sector in Norway is a highly competitive market due to high tolls and taxes, efficiency-focused prominent industry actors, and only three dominating grocery chains controlling the national market (OECD, 2020)."
- Direct-to-consumer channels enumerated for local food: farmers' markets, farm shops, Internet sales, REKO rings (REKO = Swedish "Rejäl Konsumtion", social-media-based direct sales), gourmet food stores.
- Paper 3 (market-driving): four pioneering food micro-businesses build new product categories in the competitive grocery market, expanding the market rather than outcompeting large actors.
- Empirical base across papers: Paper 1 — single embedded case, 12 farm-based meat entrepreneurs in a facilitated learning network; Paper 2 — 11 entrepreneurs from 10 micro-businesses; Paper 3 — 4 pioneering micro-businesses.
