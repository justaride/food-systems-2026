## Preserved contradictions and limitations

- The abstract stage values conflict by approximately three orders of magnitude with the detailed results; no automatic correction is authorized.
- The reported household shares are 39 and 36 percent. Arithmetic from 23,781 / 60,300 is approximately 39.44 percent, but the printed discrepancy remains open pending named review.
- Methods differ by stage, questionnaire non-response affects estimates, the household estimate is extrapolated from the Reykjavík area, and no error interval is reported.
- All values remain blocked from aggregation, external use and coverage promotion.
