## Preserved contradiction and limitations

- The three export values—19,132, 19,131 and 19,105 tonnes—remain unresolved pending an underlying table, erratum or issuer clarification.
- Category 09.1 is broader than edible food waste.
- Some weights are calculated from volume, and R3+ is a treatment classification rather than proof of a circular outcome.
- All values remain blocked from aggregation, external use and coverage promotion.
