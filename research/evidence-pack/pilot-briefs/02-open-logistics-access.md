## What is controlled

- Nordic grocery distribution is highly vertically integrated, especially in Norway and Finland, with major retail groups operating their own logistics systems.
- Menon/Kvale's 2026 functional/accounting-separation review does not recommend imposing functional or accounting separation in the grocery value chain on the evidence it reviewed.
- Menon is still useful for the pilot because it maps the policy logic: separation, access duties, non-discrimination, neutrality, transparency, and anti-cross-subsidy rules are normally considered together rather than as stand-alone tools.
- The distribution adoption-gate memo supports a system-level framing: concentrated chain-linked distribution can create adoption barriers for new food-system models, but public sources do not prove a specific BAMA/ASKO/chain refusal pattern.

## Pilotable question

Could a concept study define a limited open-logistics-access model for selected product categories or pilots, including transparent terms, service levels, data boundaries, and non-discrimination checks, without assuming that current access refusal is already proven?

## Decision options for the workshop

- **Narrow concept study:** model one product/channel such as local vegetables, CEA greens, or public-kitchen supply.
- **Transparency pilot:** request accounting or service-level transparency before any access duty is discussed.
- **Regulatory comparison:** compare grocery with telecom/power separation logic, explicitly noting Menon's warning that sector transfer is not automatic.
- **Defer:** keep logistics access as a whitepaper finding until partner interviews establish practical demand.

## Human validation asks

- Ask independent wholesalers and public buyers what actual access bottlenecks they experience: price, minimum volume, forecasting, cold chain, delivery frequency, data, or slotting.
- Ask legal reviewers whether any access model can be framed as a voluntary pilot rather than a sectoral duty.
- Ask logistics actors what data can be shared without exposing commercially sensitive route/customer information.

## Do not say

- Do not say Menon recommends functional separation; it does not.
- Do not say access refusal is proven across the sector.
- Do not say BAMA, ASKO, or any named actor unlawfully blocks vertical farming or local producers based on the current public evidence.
- Do not transfer telecom/power remedies into grocery as if the markets were equivalent.
