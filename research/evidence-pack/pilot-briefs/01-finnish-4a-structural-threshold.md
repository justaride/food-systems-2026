## What is controlled

- Finland has a grocery-specific Competition Act §4a rule that treats a grocery retailer or retail-group operator with more than 30 percent national daily consumer-goods retail share as dominant for abuse-of-dominance assessment.
- The threshold is not an automatic ban, fine, divestment trigger, or UTP/good-trading-practice rule. It is an evidentiary shortcut for dominance status; abuse still has to be assessed case by case.
- FCCA's Kesko/SOK sales-data investigation was closed without further action after the authority assessed the conduct and market context.
- The Nordic comparison does not show a shared Nordic EMV-specific concentration threshold or a common automatic grocery-concentration prohibition.

## Pilotable question

Could Norway or a Nordic forum run a structural-threshold assessment that asks: when grocery market share passes a defined level, what evidence should authorities collect by default on buyer power, EMV, wholesale/logistics access, location control, and data-sharing risk?

This is an assessment design question, not a ready legal conclusion.

## Decision options for the workshop

- **Analysis-only track:** include a roadmap action to compare Finnish §4a with Norwegian dominance tools and Swedish/Danish monitoring.
- **Evidence-trigger track:** define a threshold that triggers mandatory data collection, not automatic legal consequence.
- **No-go track:** leave structural-threshold work out of the pilot package and keep it as a whitepaper caveat.

## Human validation asks

- Ask competition-law reviewers whether the threshold can be framed as a diagnostic trigger without implying an automatic infringement.
- Ask Nordic partners whether Finnish §4a is viewed as practically useful or mostly symbolic in current enforcement.
- Ask whether the roadmap should point to national law, Nordic soft-law comparison, or an authority-methodology memo.

## Do not say

- Do not say Finland has a grocery UTP law equivalent through §4a.
- Do not say a 30 percent share is illegal in Finland.
- Do not say the Kesko/SOK closeout proves there is no competition issue in Finnish grocery.
- Do not propose a Norwegian threshold as if it were partner-approved.
