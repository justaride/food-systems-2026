# Pilot-brief evidence packs

Status: workshop-start dossiers
Last updated: 2026-07-02
Owner: Food Systems 2026 / Mission 6 preparation

These memos prepare the human pilot workshop from controlled evidence, not blank pages. They are not final policy proposals and they do not simulate partner validation. Each brief states what is known, what can be modeled or tested, what evidence is still missing, and what the workshop must not claim.

## Briefs

| Brief | Lever | Workshop use |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Finnish §4a structural competition threshold | Decide whether a Norwegian/Nordic structural-threshold assessment belongs in the roadmap as an analysis track, not an automatic sanction proposal. |
| 02 | Open logistics access | Scope a concept study for third-party access, non-discrimination, or accounting transparency in grocery logistics. |
| 03 | Zoning and establishment barriers | Convert property/location evidence into a municipal barrier-mapping pilot. |
| 04 | ASKO-Vestby dependency model | Define inputs for a logistics dependency and disruption-scenario model. |
| 05 | Municipal procurement standard | Shape a public-food procurement pilot around kitchen capability, menu design, and measurable demand. |

## Shared guardrails

- Use these as pre-read dossiers for Mission 6; do not treat them as partner-approved positions.
- Keep legal mechanisms separate: dominance threshold, UTP/good-trading-practice rules, merger control, procurement rules, and planning/zoning rules are different tools.
- Keep model outputs separate from proof of conduct. A dependency model can show exposure, not intent or unlawful blocking.
- Mark source gaps as workshop questions instead of filling them with assumptions.
