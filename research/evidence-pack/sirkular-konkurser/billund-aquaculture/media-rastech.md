Jon Refsnes, chairman of Billund Aquaculture said in a press release that there were no other options other than a closure of the business, reported Salmon Business . It was reported that the COVID-19 pandemic and the Ukraine war adversely affected the company’s finances were affected. The company’s efforts of risk management and changes in projects to secure the necessary capital for continued operations were not enough. “Based on this, the board has concluded that there is no basis for further operation,” said Refsnes. “Efforts will now be made to find the best possible solutions for all our employees, customers, and creditors.” Advertisement The shareholders, Havbruksparken Utvikling AS, Stoksund AS, and the Sørensen family, have been joint owners since the summer of 2022. Kristoffer Lund, who became the CEO of Billund Aquaculture in April, said that the company had good operations over the past year. However, challenges from previous operations made it difficult to secure sufficient capital.
