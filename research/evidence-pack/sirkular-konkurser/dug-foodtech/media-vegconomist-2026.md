The decision was made at the company’s own request after it failed to secure sufficient financing to sustain operations. Nasdaq Stockholm has since delisted the company&#8217;s shares from the Nasdaq First North Growth Market. Bankruptcy trustee Ann-Christine Halén of Ackordscentralen Syd AB has been appointed to oversee the administration. The company&#8217;s assets are now being offered for sale via a tender process. Interested parties have until January 22, 2026 to submit offers for all or parts of the business. “A chapter has now come to an end” The company&#8217;s final message to consumers and supporters was posted on social media: &#8220;For DUG, a chapter has now come to an end. With full hearts, we want to thank you for all the love and support over the years. Your passion for our drinks has been truly incredible. Now it’s time for us to say goodbye in this context.&#8221; © DUG DUG Foodtech, best known for developing a potato-based dairy alternative , had operated under the name Veg of Lund until a rebranding in mid-2024. Despite entering a competitive segment of the plant-based beverage market, the company struggled to maintain financial stability. Over the past year, its share price fell by nearly 78%, and more than 99% over the past five years. Outgoing CEO Helene Nielsen also commented publicly: &#8220;Thank you, DUG team, for now. It has been a pleasure working with you, and my best recommendations to all of you. We almost made it.&#8221; Interest from domestic and international buyers While no formal list of bidders has been made public, the bankruptcy trustee notes that interest has been expressed by both Swedish and international entities. The final decision on the sale of assets remains at the discretion of the trustee throughout the tender process. Don't miss out!
