Hooked has been facing financial difficulties for some time; according to Breakit , last year&#8217;s turnover was just 1.4 million kroner, with a loss of 6.2 million. The company was saved from bankruptcy at the last minute after contacting almost 100 investors and raising additional funding . However, it appears that this was only a short-term solution. Hooked also attempted to branch out from seafood alternatives to plant-based meat products such as kebabs and mince, but this was not successful. The company was eventually declared bankrupt earlier this week. © Hooked Foods &#8220;We pushed further than many think is possible&#8221; In a LinkedIn post , Hooked founder and CEO Tom Johansson highlighted the company&#8217;s accomplishments over the past seven years, which have included raising over $6 million in funding and gaining listings at some of the largest retailers in Sweden and Germany . Johansson said he had made several mistakes since founding the company at the age of 25, and would carry those lessons with him. He now hopes to start a new business. &#8220;We set out to solve a hard problem: making protein production better for people and planet,&#8221; said Johansson. &#8220;We pushed further than many think is possible with real products, real customers, real production, and real innovation. Thank you to each and every one who has been a part of this journey and believed in us. It has been a tough but amazing ride, I truly appreciate you all very much.&#8221; Don't miss out!
