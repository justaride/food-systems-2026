Fil­ing fi­nan­cial state­ments with the Finnish Trade Reg­is­ter
Log in to the on­line fil­ing at ytj.fi.

The law requires companies to file their financial statements with the Trade Register. Companies must also make sure that all financial statement documents required by law are being filed.

Please note: By law, the financial statements must be filed even if the company is inactive, or it has not conducted any actual business during the financial period.

File the financial statements with the Trade Register when they have been approved. File your notification online using our service at ytj.fi in Finnish and Swedish.

Did your company receive a letter about late financial statements?

File the financial statements online immediately using our service at ytj.fi. See our instructions on filing. Read more in our frequently asked questions about late financial statements.​

Which companies must file financial statements with the Trade Register?

Most companies must file their financial statements with the Trade Register.

See the timeline below to check when to file your financial statements. Go to our instructions via the links below or the menu.

Which companies do not need to file?

The following companies do not need to file their financial statements with the Trade Register:

  • housing companies;
  • tenant-owners' societies;
  • housing co-operatives; and
  • mutual real estate limited companies.

Usually, private traders do not need to file their financial statements with the Trade Register.

What does it cost?

​Filing financial statements by the deadline is free of charge if you use the online service at ytj.fi. The online service is available in Finnish and Swedish.

The PRH imposes a fee for financial statements filed late with the Finnish Trade Register. Read more: Fee for financial statements filed late.

How do I know if the financial statements have been registered?

​As soon as the financial statements have been registered, a register extract will be sent to the company’s mailing address recorded in the Finnish Business Information System at ytj.fi.

Run a search in our Virre Information Service free of charge to see which of your company’s financial statements have already been recorded in the Finnish Trade Register. Go to the Virre Information ServiceAvautuu uuteen välilehteen.
