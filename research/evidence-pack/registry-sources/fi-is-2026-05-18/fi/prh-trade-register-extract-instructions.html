Trade Reg­is­ter ex­tract

A Trade Register extract includes the company’s current registered details.

You can download Trade Register extracts to your computer free of charge from the Virre Information Service

  1. Go to the Virre Information ServiceAvautuu uuteen välilehteen
  2. Enter the Finnish Business ID of the company on the front page of the service.
  3. The Company Information page shows basic details that are free of charge. Select “Open a Trade Register extract free of charge” to access a Trade Register extract free of charge.
  4. Print out or save the extract to your device.
  5. If you do not know the Finnish Business ID of the company, you can search for the company by name.

​Read more in our instructions on how to buy and order a Trade Register extract in English.​

History extract from the Finnish Trade Register

You can also download a free Trade Register extract from the Virre Information Service for a certain period of time.

If the company was registered in the Trade Register after 1 September 1992, the Trade Register extract will contain information from the company’s registration date onwards.If the company was registered earlier, you will find the starting date of the company’s information in the system by searching for the company.

  1. Go to “Extracts, certificates and organisation rules” in Virre.
  2. Enter the Business ID.
  3. An electronic Trade Register extract is pre-selected in the service.
  4. Select “I want to have an extract for a specific day or for a certain period of time” and enter the dates.
  5. Accept the selected dates, and the electronic register extract will open.
  6. Print out or save the extract to your device.

Order extracts and pay by invoice

You can also order Trade Register extracts and pay for them by invoice. In such a case, the price of a Trade Register extract is 15 euros. An invoicing fee of 6.50 euros is added. For delivery by post, we charge a delivery fee of 5.50 euros.

If you place an order as a private person and not on behalf of a company or other organisation, write your personal identity code for invoicing on the order form under “Other information”.
