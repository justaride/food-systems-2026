## Scope and mode

`/prosjektlandskap` is an internal Operate/Read surface.

## Audience and job

Food Systems Transition Group members compare project references, identify Nordic and thematic gaps, and decide which evidence or candidate requires the next review action.

## Task and proof

The reader should see coverage before rank, compare strategic priority with evidence strength, filter the full 40-profile register, and inspect the gated candidate queue. All values are derived from the dated JSONL registers.

## Constraints

Preserve the incumbent interface and status language. No database import, candidate promotion, external publication, contact, or implied independent evaluation. Completion-register remains canonical for gates.

## Direction and memorable moment

Coverage matrix first: a nine-cell Nordic and Sápmi field controls the full register, paired with thematic depth. The memorable moment is selecting a thin geography and immediately seeing which verified profiles actually carry that cell.

## Unresolved decisions

Whether a later dated landscape should replace the 2026-08-10 projection, and whether project detail pages should be added after the register has more independent evaluations.
