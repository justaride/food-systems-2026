Research Methodology
• Representative online samples of approximately 1,000 adults in each of
  31 markets (500 each in Hong Kong, Kenya, Nigeria, and Singapore), and
  1,500 in USA (n=29,565).

• Samples representative of online population, weighted to reflect general
  population census data.

• Online surveying in July and August 2023.

• Where historical data are shown, questions were asked using an in-person
  and telephone methodology before 2019.




Introduction
EAT and GlobeScan wanted to conduct significant research on sustainable
food systems. Since 2021, the two organizations have collaborated yearly
on developing questions to the public in 31 markets around the world about
their definitions of good, healthy, and sustainable food, their concerns about
the current food system, and who should drive the changes needed
to improve it.

This report presents the insights gained from this research, with the hope
of contributing to the transformation of the global food system.
