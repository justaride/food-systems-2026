Oslo Youth for Healthy
and Sustainable Diets
This report summarizes the EAT Oslo Youth for Healthy and Sustainable
Diets project, which invited local youth and other stakeholders to share
recommendations on how public spaces and school meals could be
reconsidered to enhance health and sustainability.
October 2022




This project was supported by the Research Council of Norway.
The Norwegian name for the project is Ung og sulten i Oslo:
Løsninger for et sunt og bærekraftig kosthold.
