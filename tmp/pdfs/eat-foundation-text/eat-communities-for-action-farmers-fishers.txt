02.5

Stop wasteful and destructive fishing practices.

Cease the use of illegal or destructive fishing gear and
minimise to the highest degree possible the levels of
bycatch and fish waste. These practices deplete marine
ecosystems, waste valuable nutrition, and threaten the
livelihoods of small-scale fishing communities.
