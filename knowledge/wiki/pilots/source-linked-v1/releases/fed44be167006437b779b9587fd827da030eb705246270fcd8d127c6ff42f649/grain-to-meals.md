# From grain reserves to meals: a first Norwegian explanation

As of: 2026-09-15. Internal candidate preview — not reviewed, canonical, or approved for external use.

## Scope

Food wheat in Norway, from storage through conversion and distribution to recipients. Evidence checked on 15 September 2026. A bounded candidate synthesis building on A1, not a full systematic review, operational forecast or national readiness assessment.

Stored grain becomes useful food only through connected functions: release and allocation; quality selection and handling; milling; packaging or bulk delivery; baking or other preparation; distribution; and access by recipients. ### grain-countercase · inference · candidate_linked

The reserve addresses one form of exposure: grain availability. If downstream conversion and delivery remain functional, stock can address a raw-material interruption. If those functions cannot operate, a larger nominal stock alone does not establish more delivered meals. The relevant test is performance under a stated disruption, not the stock headline alone.

Boundary: Conditional reasoning, not a prediction of failure. Additional imports, alternative facilities, substitutions and recovery could alter the outcome.
