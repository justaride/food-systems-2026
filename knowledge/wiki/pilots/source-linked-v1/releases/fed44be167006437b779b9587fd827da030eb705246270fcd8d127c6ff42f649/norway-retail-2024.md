# Norwegian grocery concentration: what the 2024 numbers mean

As of: 2026-09-15. Internal candidate preview — not reviewed, canonical, or approved for external use.

## Scope

A historical Norway-only measurement example for source-to-calculation-to-explanation updates. No current market, causality or Nordic-comparability claim.

## Evidence-linked explanation

### retail-definition · source_report · candidate_linked

This historical comparison covers traditional Norwegian grocery retail with physical sales from stores; it excludes online retail from the authority’s reported scope.

Boundary: Not all food purchases, Nordic retail, local catchment competition, or a current-2026 market measure.

### retail-concentration · calculation · candidate_linked

Using the published rounded 2024 revenue shares, the reconstructed HHI is 3327 and CR3 is 96.6%. HHI is an index; CR3 is a percentage.

Boundary: Sum squared percentage shares, round HHI to an integer, and sum the three largest shares for CR3. Inputs sum to 99.9% through rounding; no renormalisation. Compare with existing CA-004, without changing its review status.

### retail-interpretation · inference · candidate_linked

These measures describe the distribution of turnover. They do not identify the cause of prices, prove unlawful conduct, or measure resilience. Those would require separate evidence and definitions.

Boundary: Analytical boundary, not a causal finding or legal assessment.
