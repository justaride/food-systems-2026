# Aktørcaser – suksess og fiasko

> Casesamling · Del av [[Innsiktskartet]]

Suksess- og fiaskocaser fra sirkulær matøkonomi — mønstrene bak hva som skalerer.

## Suksess

- **Too Good To Go** (DK, R1) — Surplus food app — discounted 'magic bags' from restaurants/stores _Lærdom: Two-sided plattform som løser både husholdningsøkonomi og klimanød: billig mat + konkret svinnreduksjon. Transaksjonsbasert modell gir skalerbar enhetøkonomi._
- **Solar Foods (Solein)** (FI, R1) — Protein from CO2 + electricity via gas fermentation _Lærdom: Deep-tech can scale with patient capital + regulatory fast-track (Singapore)_
- **Den Magiske Fabrikken** (NO, R9) — Co-digestion of food waste + manure for biogas + fertilizer _Lærdom: Public-private partnership + waste infrastructure integration works_
- **REKO-ringer** (nordic, R1) — Facebook-based direct sale from local producers to consumers _Lærdom: Zero infrastructure costs (Facebook groups). Grassroots scales when friction is minimal_
- **Gasum** (nordic, R9) — Cross-Nordic biogas production and LBG distribution _Lærdom: Nordic interoperability in energy infrastructure enables scale_

## Fiasko

- **Restaurant Rest** (NO) — Zero-waste gourmet restaurant (8 tables), Michelin Green Star _Lærdom: Premium/fine dining + circularity = economically fragile. High fixed costs cannot absorb macro shocks_
- **Enorm Biofactory** (DK) — BSF insect protein factory (22,000 m²) for aquaculture and livestock feed, converting organic waste streams to protein meal _Lærdom: Unngå single-asset risiko i umodne markeder. Modulær oppskalering (flere små anlegg) hadde gitt inntekt underveis og redusert total eksponering mot én teknologisk flaskehals._

## Mønster

Platform/discount models (TGTG, REKO, Holdbart) survive under cost pressure. Production-heavy models (Enorm, Ynsect, Infarm) collapse — even with €600M+ invested. Key failure patterns: (1) mega-CAPEX in unproven markets, (2) B2C plant-meat post-hype demand collapse, (3) structural price gap vs soy/fishmeal (3-10x). Survivors: B2B ingredient plays (PeelPioneers), consortium/foundation models (AX Foundation), low-CAPEX platforms (TGTG, Matsentralen). Finnish insect companies (Volare) surviving where Danish/French fail — leaner cost structure + patient capital.

**Strøm:** Industrial potatoes (fries, chips, gratins) → peeling/trimming → peels and off-cuts → livestock feed (cattle, pigs) and biogas

- R-nivå: **R7** · TRL 9 · Modenhet: **industrial**
- Volum: 20,000-40,000 t/yr potato peels and trim in Nordics. 15-25% of industrial potato becomes waste/sidestream
- Aktører: HOFF, Findus, Strand Unikorn, Orkla, regional biogas plants
- Politikkstøtte: Market-driven. Potato classified as waste/residue under biofuel regulations (Miljodirektoratet). No dedicated upgrading policy
- Tema: `sidestream` · Kilder: Perplexity sidestromskartlegging 20.04.26, DI Foedevarer 13 sidestrommes 2024, Regjeringen potetplakat, Miljodirektoratet ravare-klassifisering

# Loop – Rapskake til dyrefor (Norden)

> Sirkulær loop (nordic) · Del av [[Innsiktskartet]]

**Strøm:** Rapeseed → oil pressing → rapeseed cake (35-40% protein) → livestock feed (replacing imported soy)

- R-nivå: **R7** · TRL 9 · Modenhet: **industrial**
- Volum: Several hundred thousand tonnes/yr across Nordics. Rapeseed cake = ~2/3 of seed weight after pressing
- Aktører: AAK, Felleskjopet, norske rapsolje-produsenter (RO m.fl.)
- Politikkstøtte: Market-driven. DI Foedevarer lists oilseed presscake among top 13 sidestreams. Norwegian government promotes domestic rapeseed cultivation
- Tema: `sidestream` · Kilder: Perplexity sidestromskartlegging 20.04.26, DI Foedevarer pressekake 2024, Coop RO rapsolje, Felleskjopet kornstatistikk 2024-25
