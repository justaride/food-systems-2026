# Cover note - Food transfer review package

Date: 2026-05-26

## What this is

This package isolates Food-relevant details from Circular Cities/JT May work so Food Systems can review them in its own process.

It is not a formal Food TG decision, not external validation, and not a claim that Food Systems has already adopted the method.

## Why this belongs in Food

The relevant material is about how to assess circularity in food and biomass flows:

- whether edible material remains food for humans
- whether non-edible material can become feed or ingredient value
- whether side streams are upcycled before they become compost, nutrients, biogas or energy
- whether case selection should use a broader evaluation lens than private profitability alone

That is Food Systems territory. Cities should only keep the interface where food/bio flows affect city systems: waste, wastewater, procurement, utilities, spatial planning and symbiosis.

## What Food gets from this package

Food gets a bounded review package:

- a source map to the Circular evidence
- a Wageningen/Moerman/R9 method brief
- a Food biomass cascade
- a 16-field case-evaluation template
- review questions for Food ownership
- apply and verification steps for a writable Food session

## Current status

Food-relevant material from the Cities/JT audit is ready for transfer as an internal evidence package, but it is not yet Food Systems source-registered or externally validated.

## What Food should not do

- Do not paste the Circular meeting note into Food as final evidence without a Food-local intake note.
- Do not mark Wageningen/Moerman as approved Food TG method before source locators and claim links exist.
- Do not treat the Ghana compost example as Nordic proof.
- Do not treat a higher cascade placement as pilot readiness.
- Do not use "sirkulaert" for compost, biogass or energy without explaining why higher-value use is not possible or not documented.

## Suggested acceptance label

If Food accepts this as an internal package, use this label:

> Accepted as internal Food method-intake. Source registration, direct Wageningen/Cathrine locator evidence and Food TG validation remain open.
