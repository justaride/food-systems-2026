# Method brief - Wageningen/Moerman/R9 for Food Systems

Date: 2026-05-26

## Method proposition

Food should use Moermans ladder plus R9 as a Food-specific value-retention method. R9 gives the general circularity vocabulary. Moerman gives the food-specific hierarchy: edible material should remain human food before it becomes feed, industrial input, nutrient product, compost, energy or leakage.

Wageningen should be treated as a case-evaluation lens until Food has registered exact page/table/figure locators, claim links and Food TG decision status. Food already has a Wageningen PDF/DOI source candidate as `SRC-B-035`; the remaining gap is locator and claim closure, not absence of any source candidate.

## Food biomass cascade

| Rank | Level | Food interpretation | Gate before strong claim |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prevention | Avoid surplus, overproduction and quality loss before waste arises. | Baseline, category, time window, counterfactual. |
| 2 | Human food / redistribution | Keep edible food as food for people. | Edibility, quality, recipient, time window, food safety. |
| 3 | Ingredient / upcycling | Upgrade side streams into new food or ingredient value. | Stream owner, hygiene, stabilization, legal end use, buyer. |
| 4 | Feed | Use safe biomass as animal or fish feed when human food is not possible. | Substrate gate, ABP/TSE, feed rules, maturity, buyer. |
| 5 | Industrial bioresource | Use biological streams in materials, fermentation, chemistry or other industrial value. | Product status, market, technology, system boundary, off-taker. |
| 6 | Nutrient recovery / soil | Compost, digestate, struvite or other N/P/K loops when higher use is not realistic. | N/P/K, contaminants, product status, rules, market. |
| 7 | Energy | Biogas or incineration where higher value is impossible or undocumented. | Explain why human food, feed, ingredient, industry or nutrients are not possible. |
| 8 | Leakage / disposal | Landfill, unmanaged loss or value loss without documented resource use. | Treat as gap, not circular success. |

## What the method should do

For every empirical Food finding, ask:

1. Where is the stream today?
2. Is it edible, feed-safe, ingredient-grade, industrial, nutrient-grade, energy-only, or leakage?
3. Can it move upward in the cascade?
4. What would block that move: law, hygiene, contamination, volume, logistics, buyer, data, cost or governance?
5. What evidence is needed before Food can say anything externally?

## 16-field case-evaluation template

This is a Food-adapted operational template. It is not a verbatim copy of Wageningen Table 1. The original Wageningen 16 indicators are four circularity indicators, four socio-economic indicators, four environmental indicators and four implementability indicators; Food can map those into the fields below when the source locator is locked.

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Stream type | What is the stream, and is it edible, feed-safe, biological, mixed or contaminated? |
| Current use | Where in the cascade is the stream today? |
| Upward shift | Which higher cascade level can it realistically move to? |
| R-level | Which R-level describes the intervention, and does generic R logic fit food? |
| Volume and frequency | Are tonnes, batch size, seasonality, consistency and data source known? |
| Quality and risk | Are hygiene, microbiology, contaminants and shelf-life known? |
| Legal end use | Is food, feed, fertilizer, digestate or industrial use legally documented? |
| Owner and data owner | Who owns the stream, data and operation? |
| Buyer/off-taker | Who will buy or use the output? |
| Implementation | Which technology, logistics, standard and operating model are needed? |
| Economics | Is private profitability documented, or does the case rely on public/system value? |
| System benefit | Which jobs, soil quality, resilience, biodiversity, climate or city effects matter? |
| Baseline and counterfactual | What happens to the stream without intervention? |
| Transferability | What is Nordic, local or case-specific? |
| Claim status | Is this benchmark, hypothesis, pilot candidate, secondary track or hold-back? |
| Next gate | Is the next step source locator, primary check, actor validation, C-gate or scope decision? |
